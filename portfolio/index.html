    01 · 背景与目标Brief and goals

    换乘中的三个问题，行程规划应用很少一起回答Three questions in a transfer that planners rarely answer together

    TripView 希望探索换乘辅助功能。出发前能看路线和时刻表；换乘的几分钟里，用户问的是另一组问题。TripView wanted to explore a transfer assistance feature. Before departure riders see routes and timetables; during a transfer they ask different questions.

    1. 1我还赶得上下一班吗？Can I make the next service?出发倒计时 · 步行时间 · 剩余时间Countdown · walk time · spare time
    2. 2我还能准时到达吗？Will I still arrive on time?以活动开始时间为基准，而非车辆Measured against the event, not the vehicle
    3. 3不行的话现在做什么？If not, what now?排好序、可立即开始的替代方案A ranked alternative that can start at once

    03站内引导Station guidance地标、方向图示、短句；可重新确认位置Landmarks, direction graphics, short copy; re-confirm position
    04延误与改道Delays and rerouting显示延误幅度与对到达的影响，可等可换Show delay and effect on arrival; wait or switch

    访谈提纲（节选）Interview guide (excerpt)
    • 带我走一遍你最近一次通勤，从出门开始。Walk me through your most recent commute, from leaving the house.
    • 讲一次换乘出问题的经历：你做了什么，感受如何，之后有没有改变出行方式？Tell me about a time a transfer went badly. What did you do, and did it change how you travel?

    03 · 用户画像与旅程Persona and journey

    旅程的低谷在换乘，设计因此聚焦第 3 阶段The journey bottoms out at the transfer, so the design focuses on phase 3

    画像：Jamie Park，21 岁，悉尼外围通勤者Persona: Jamie Park, 21, outer-Sydney commuter

    “每次换乘都是一次小赌博。我有自己的办法，但一条错误信息就能毁掉整个早晨。Every transfer is a small gamble. I have my own tricks, but one bad piece of information can undo my whole morning.”

    • 每周 3–4 天通勤，每趟 1–3 次换乘3–4 days a week, 1–3 transfers per trip
    • 出发前、车上、换乘前都查应用，并与实物标识核对Checks the app before leaving, on board and before each transfer, and cross-checks signage
    • 核心张力：例行与掌控，对比突如其来的不确定Core tension: routine and control versus sudden uncertainty
    体验故事：多米诺效应Experience story: the domino effect

    应用显示换乘“准点”，列车实际晚了 2 分钟却没有更新。3 分钟的缓冲不到 1 分钟，Jamie 只能随人群冲刺。让人恼火的是，每天依赖的工具在最关键的时刻给了错误的信心。The app says “On Time”, but the train is two minutes late and nothing updates. A three-minute buffer drops under one and Jamie sprints with the crowd. The frustration is false confidence from the daily tool at the moment it mattered.

    04 · 概念选择Concept selection

    两个概念对照研究低谷，选择决策层并并入站内引导Two concepts against the research low point; the decision layer wins, with guidance folded in

    概念 1 · Transfer Coach 决策层Concept 1 · Transfer Coach, a decision layer

    把实时数据转成一个明确的下一步，服务上车前 3–10 分钟。流程：监测 → 预警 → 指引 → 恢复。Turns live data into one confident next move for the 3–10 minutes before boarding. Flow: monitor → warn → direct → recover.

    概念 2 · Station Flow 步行引导Concept 2 · Station Flow, walking guidance

    用指南针与步数在每个转弯提示，可振动或耳机语音，覆盖步行段。流程：检测 → 定向 → 引导 → 确认。Uses compass and step count for turn cues, with vibration or earbuds, for the walk. Flow: detect → orient → guide → confirm.

    设计原则Design principles
    • 状态优先：当前状态与下一步永远最大Status first: current state and next action are always largest
    • 两个时钟：下一班车与目的地时间分开Two clocks: next service and destination stay separate

    07 · 成果与反思Outcomes and reflection

    从功能到时刻，从观点到证据，从可用到有感受From features to moments, opinion to evidence, usable to felt

    1

    从功能到时刻From features to moments

    以换乘的 3–10 分钟这一个真实时刻为起点，而不是功能清单。Started from one real moment, the 3–10 minutes of a transfer, not a feature list.

    2

    从观点到证据From opinion to evidence

    专家评审、7 次测试与 2 份日志使屏幕改了四轮，每处改动都有出处。The review, seven sessions and two journals changed the screens four times; each change has a source.

    3

    从可用到有感受From usable to felt

    好用的应用仍会让人焦虑。体验评估发现了任务测试漏掉的压力。An easy app can still cause anxiety. Experience evaluation found stress that task testing missed.
